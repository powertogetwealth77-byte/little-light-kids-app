# Little Light Kids™ Brand Rules

## Identity

Little Light Kids™ is a Jesus-centered children’s story platform.

The brand must feel like a real premium children’s media universe, not church homework, generic Sunday-school filler, or fear-based religious content.

## Brand standard

Every story, app screen, image prompt, prayer, character, and product decision must be:

- Biblically faithful
- Jesus-centered
- Emotionally strong
- Child-safe
- Parent-trusted
- Visually premium
- Spiritually clean
- Warm, hopeful, and courageous
- Clear enough for children to understand
- Strong enough for parents to trust

## Forbidden tone / content

Do not use:

- New Age language
- Manifestation language
- Occult or magic framing
- Vague spirituality
- Fear-based manipulation
- Scary spectacle designed to frighten children
- Generic moralism without Jesus/truth
- Corny filler language
- Brand mixing from Seer AI, DOMOS, RFMG, or Christian Surfers

## Story balance rule

Target story composition:

- 70% story, emotion, friendship, adventure, tension, wonder, and character movement
- 30% biblical lesson, correction, scripture, prayer, and spiritual application

## Core phrases

Approved recurring language:

- Jesus is the Light.
- Choose the Light.
- Walk in the truth.
- I shine bright.
- Jesus leads me.

## Character system rule

The app must support the full verified Little Light Kids™ character roster and future characters. Do not hardcode the product around only Zeke, King Light, or The Whisper.

Characters need:

- Name
- Role
- Kid-safe description
- Traits
- Approved themes
- Restricted themes
- Asset references
- Approval status
- Notes on visual consistency

## Public app vs internal factory

Little Light Kids™ public app:

- Story library
- Reader
- Characters
- Parent dashboard
- Prayers
- Subscription/customer experience

Little Light Forge / Book Factory:

- Internal admin production system
- Character registry management
- Asset library
- Book creation workflow
- Visual QA
- Export packages

Do not mix internal operator tooling into the public children’s app unless it is behind protected admin routes.
